## 4. Measuring Tips & Fit Assurance
- **Chest:** Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal under the arms.
- **Waist:** Measure around your natural waistline, where your trousers comfortably sit.
- **Between Sizes?** If your measurements fall between two sizes, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed fit, or sizing down for a tailored slim fit.
- **Free Size Exchange:** Remember, the first size exchange on any apparel or footwear order is **100% free** within 30 days!
