# SMK E-Commerce Shipping & Delivery Guide

**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Document ID:** SMK-DOC-SHIP-002  
**Applies to:** Pan-India Deliveries on SMK Store

---

## 1. Shipping Zones & Delivery Timelines

SMK fulfills orders from regional fulfillment hubs across India to ensure the fastest delivery turnaround:

| Region / Tier | Coverage | Estimated Transit Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Zone 1: Metro Cities** | Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune | **1–3 business days** |
| **Zone 2: Tier 1 & Tier 2 Cities** | Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, Chandigarh, Kochi, Indore, Surat | **3–5 business days** |
| **Zone 3: Rest of India (Tier 3 & Rural)** | District headquarters and standard postal pincodes | **4–7 business days** |
| **Zone 4: Special / Remote Regions** | Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, North Eastern states, Andaman & Nicobar | **7–10 business days** |

*Note: Business days exclude Sundays and gazetted national holidays.*

---

## 2. Shipping Charges & Thresholds

- **Free Standard Shipping:** On all domestic orders with a subtotal value of **₹999 or above**.
- **Standard Shipping Fee:** A flat fee of **₹79** applies to orders below ₹999.
- **Express Same-Day / Next-Day Delivery:** Available in select metro pin codes (Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Mumbai) for an additional **₹149** on orders placed before 12:00 PM IST.

---

## 3. Cash on Delivery (COD) Rules
- **Eligibility:** Available for orders up to a maximum cart value of **₹5,000**.
- **COD Convenience Fee:** A flat fee of **₹49** is applied to cover third-party courier cash collection and handling.
- **Verification:** First-time COD orders may require a quick WhatsApp OTP confirmation or automated call verification before dispatch.
- **Pincode Serviceability:** Over 19,000+ Indian postal codes are serviced for COD. If your area is unserviceable for COD, prepaid payment via UPI or Card is required.

---

## 4. Courier Partners & Tracking
All shipments are dispatched via verified tier-1 logistics partners:
- **Primary Courier Partners:** BlueDart Express, Delhivery, XpressBees, DTDC, and Shadowfax.
- **Real-Time Tracking:** Upon dispatch from our warehouse, you will receive an SMS and email notification with an active AWB tracking number.
- **Tracking URL:** You can track shipment milestones live at [https://smk-tan.vercel.app/orders](https://smk-tan.vercel.app/orders) or directly on the courier partner's portal.

---

## 5. Delivery Attempts & Non-Delivery (RTO)
- Courier executives attempt delivery up to **3 consecutive times**.
- If delivery fails due to incorrect address, customer unavailability, or refusal to accept COD:
  - The package is marked as **Return to Origin (RTO)**.
  - For prepaid orders returned as RTO, the order amount minus ₹79 handling fee is automatically credited back to your original payment method.
  - Repeated artificial refusals of COD shipments may result in temporary suspension of the COD facility for the customer account.
