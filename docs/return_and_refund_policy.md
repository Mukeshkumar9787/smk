# SMK E-Commerce Return, Exchange & Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Document ID:** SMK-DOC-RET-001  
**Applies to:** All purchases on SMK Store ([smk-tan.vercel.app](https://smk-tan.vercel.app/))

---

## 1. Overview & Return Window
At SMK, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We offer a **30-day hassle-free return and exchange window** from the date of confirmed delivery for eligible products.

- **Standard Return Window:** 30 calendar days from delivery date.
- **Defective / Damaged / Wrong Item Received:** Report within **48 hours** of delivery with unboxing photos/videos for expedited priority replacement.

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for a return or exchange:
1. The product must be unused, unwashed, and undamaged.
2. All original tags, barcodes, warranty cards, manuals, and protective packaging must be intact.
3. The original invoice or proof of purchase must accompany the package.
4. Footwear must be tried indoors and returned in the undamaged original shoe box.

### Non-Returnable Items:
- Intimate apparel, innerwear, swimwear, and socks (for hygiene reasons).
- Customized, personalized, or made-to-order items.
- Final clearance items explicitly marked as **"Final Sale - Non-Returnable"**.
- Perishable goods or gift cards.

---

## 3. How to Initiate a Return or Exchange
1. Log in to your customer account at [https://smk-tan.vercel.app/my-account](https://smk-tan.vercel.app/my-account).
2. Select the order containing the item you wish to return.
3. Click **"Request Return / Exchange"**.
4. Select the reason for return and choose either:
   - **Replacement / Exchange:** Select new size or color.
   - **Refund:** Select refund method.
5. Our courier partner (BlueDart, Delhivery, or XpressBees) will schedule a reverse pickup from your delivery address within **24–48 hours**.

---

## 4. Refund Processing & Timelines
Once the returned package reaches our fulfillment center and passes quality inspection (usually within 24 hours of arrival):

| Original Payment Method | Refund Mode | Processing Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Razorpay (Credit / Debit Card)** | Original Card | 5–7 business days |
| **UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)** | Original UPI VPA | 24–48 hours |
| **Net Banking** | Bank Account | 3–5 business days |
| **Cash on Delivery (COD)** | NEFT / IMPS / UPI to verified account | 3–5 business days after bank details provided |
| **Store Credit / Gift Voucher** | Instant Wallet Credit | Immediate (< 1 hour) |

---

## 5. Reverse Shipping Charges
- **Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Product:** **100% Free reverse pickup** and replacement.
- **Change of Mind / Standard Return:** A nominal reverse logistics fee of **₹99** is deducted from the final refund amount.
- **Size / Color Exchange:** **Free of charge** for the first exchange per order.

---

## 6. Escalations & Disputes
If your refund is delayed beyond 7 business days or you dispute an inspection decision:
- Contact our dedicated support desk at **support@smk.store** with your Order ID.
- Live chat with our AI Support Assistant via the widget on any store page.
